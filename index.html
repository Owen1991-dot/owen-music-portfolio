Biography

Lim Jia Jun (Owen) is a Malaysian musician and music producer based in Kuala Lumpur, born in Muar, Johor. Driven by a lifelong passion for music, his work spans songwriting, composition, song arrangement, recording, and audio post-production, including mixing and mastering.

Owen enjoys being involved throughout the creative process — from shaping the first musical idea to developing an arrangement and refining the final sound. His portfolio includes original melody and lyric composition as well as arrangement, recording, mixing, and mastering for a range of released music projects.

Through each project, he aims to bring musical ideas to life with thoughtful arrangements, expressive sound, and attention to detail.
